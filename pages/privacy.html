The short version

Three boundaries, made visible.

The public portfolio works without an account. Personalization, sign-in, and aggregate analytics each have a different boundary.

01 · Your browser

Preferences stay local.

Your theme, optional name and goal, and saved items use browser storage on this device.

No account required
02 · Google

Identity is optional.

Opening the account panel can load Google's sign-in control. Google returns standard OpenID profile claims after you choose an account; this site ignores the email claim, keeps only your name and profile image for the browser session, and does not store the ID token.

Session only
03 · Cloudflare

Traffic stays aggregate.

On the live GitHub Pages site, Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits and performance without adding analytics cookies.

Production only
