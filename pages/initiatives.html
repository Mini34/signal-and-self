Design principle

Make the responsible action easier to repeat.

Every initiative starts with a behavior, a barrier, and one useful change—not a feature list.

01 · Behavior

What should become easier?

Define the useful action in plain language before building.

02 · Barrier

What gets in the way?

Look for friction, confusing defaults, missing context, or social pressure.

03 · Evidence

How will I know?

Pair progress with a reflection, artifact, or observable change.
